## Objective
Draft the manuscript with bounded claims and journal-aware framing.

## Detailed steps
1. Draft the outline before full section writing.
2. Frame the narrative around virtual-cell promise, random-split insufficiency, context-stress testing, structured transfer decay, PTL reliability prediction, and selective abstention.
3. Write LaTeX manuscript files with figure and table references.
4. Keep claims bounded: no clinical recommendations, wet-lab validation, universal best model, de novo drug discovery, or final biological truth.
5. Create target cover letters for EAAI, KBS, and AIME.
6. Update tracking files and task log.

## Commands
No compilation in this phase unless needed for local sanity checks. Full compilation is Phase 11.

## Useful plugin/tool support
- scientific-writing and citation-management skills for bounded narrative and reference handling.
- scite literature MCP for any factual or literature claim that needs verification.
- venue-templates skill if journal-specific formatting guidance is needed.

## Logging requirements
- Save writing decisions and unresolved citation gaps in docs/task_logs/phase10_manuscript_writing.md.

## Acceptance criteria
- Full manuscript draft exists.
- Figures and tables are referenced.
- Limitations are explicit.
- Claims are bounded.
- Cover letters exist.

## Next task dependency
Phase 11 compiles and packages the manuscript.
