\section{Ordering estimands and finite-sample identities}
For perturbation pair $c=(p,q)$, context $e$, and measurement replicate $r$,
the fixed-predictor risk difference defines
\[
 Z_{c,e}^{(r)}=\operatorname{sign}(R_{p,e}^{(r)}-R_{q,e}^{(r)})
 \in\{-1,0,+1\}.
\]
The ordering distribution retains ties:
\[
 \pi_{c,e}=[\Pr(Z=-1),\Pr(Z=0),\Pr(Z=+1)].
\]
For contexts $e_1,e_2$,
\[
 D_{\rm cross}=\mathbb{E}_c[1-\pi_{c,e_1}^{\mathsf T}\pi_{c,e_2}],\quad
 D_{\rm within,e}=\mathbb{E}_c[1-\|\pi_{c,e}\|_2^2].
\]
Expanding the squared distance yields
\[
 D_{\rm cross}-\frac{D_{\rm within,e_1}+D_{\rm within,e_2}}{2}
 =\frac12\mathbb{E}_c\|\pi_{c,e_1}-\pi_{c,e_2}\|_2^2\geq0.
\]
This is a distributional identity, not a causal effect. In finite samples the
primary within-context estimate is the leave-one-replicate-out U-statistic
\[
 \widehat D_{\rm within}^{U}=1-
 \frac{n_{-}(n_{-}-1)+n_{0}(n_{0}-1)+n_{+}(n_{+}-1)}{n(n-1)}.
\]
The operational joint-floor excess is
$D_{\rm cross}-\max(D_{\rm joint,e_1},D_{\rm joint,e_2})$. The joint surface is
empirically crossed and is not assumed to be additive.

The strict inversion diagnostic excludes tied pairs only in its denominator.
The continuous secondary displacement is
\[
 d_p=\frac{|\operatorname{rank}_{e_1}(R_p)-\operatorname{rank}_{e_2}(R_p)|}{n-1}.
\]
None of these secondary quantities changes the primary metric, context or
budget after results are observed.

\section{Frozen Frangieh protocol}
The shared Frangieh surface contains 243 perturbation labels across control,
co-culture and IFN$\gamma$, scored over 8,229 genes. A global five-fold
perturbation-label assignment is fixed before fitting. For each source context,
the source-frozen predictor is fit once per fold and its prediction vector is
held fixed while target raw-cell outcomes are resampled. The three pinned
metrics are delta cosine, Systema centroid accuracy and absolute-effect rank
agreement. The primary audit uses 30 seeds, three model members and a minimum
of 20 cells; the $n\geq40$ and original split-half runs are sensitivity
analyses, not replacement endpoints.

For each context, perturbation and threshold, with-replacement raw-cell
pseudoreplicates use the matched available count. The measurement-only floor
keeps the predictor fixed. The model-only floor keeps outcomes fixed and
crosses independent members. The joint floor crosses independent members and
measurement replicates. The exact identity residual is checked for every row;
the source table records the residual and the input lineage.

Stable ordering is cross-fit with 15 selection and 15 disjoint evaluation seeds,
with the two halves swapped. A pair is stable only when the 95\% Beta posterior
interval for its strict direction, conditional on non-ties, is entirely on one
side of 0.5 and at least eight strict observations support that direction.
Selection-stable, held-out-evaluable and held-out-inversion fractions are
descriptive and cannot choose the headline metric.

\section{Measurement depth}
The matched-fixed depth summary uses 10, 20, 40, 80, 160 and full cells per
pseudoreplicate. The perturbation universe is frozen at the highest fixed
budget and reused at lower budgets. For each source-by-contrast cell,
\[
 R(n)=D_{\rm meas\text{-}ID}(n)/D_{\rm meas\text{-}ID}(\mathrm{full}).
\]
Raw ratios above one remain visible. The resolution summary is the first budget
within ten percent of the full value. Detectability is the first budget with a
positive 90\% seed-bootstrap lower interval; no threshold is encoded as
unavailable. The lower curves in Figure 3 are compact descriptive summaries of
cell-specific values and do not create a universal cutoff. The exact
source-by-contrast rows and thresholds are in the Figure 3 source table.

\section{Decision consequence and fixed primary association}
At budgets $k/n\in\{0.05,0.10,0.20,0.50\}$, source risk selects the set and target
risk evaluates it. Retention, target regret, normalized regret and boundary
inversion use deterministic tie handling. The finite inversion check requires
$V_k\geq m^2/[k(n-k)]$ when $m$ items are mis-selected under strict ties.

The Figure 4 link is fixed before reading its value: full measurement depth,
decision budget 0.10, $x=D_{\rm meas\text{-}ID}$ and
$y=\text{normalized regret}$. Its unit is six directed biological transfers
times three metrics, exactly 18 points. The canonical decision summary has
2,000 synchronized perturbation-label-by-measurement-seed draws for each
group--budget key. Figure 4 displays those group-level interval endpoints and
labels the aggregation; it does not pool incompatible intervals. The overall
Spearman value is 0.7998 with cluster-bootstrap $q_{.05}=0.7388$ and
$q_{.95}=0.9003$. This is descriptive, not causal. Metric-specific values are
reported in the JSON report and are not headline significance tests because
each metric has only six transfers.

\section{Failure anatomy and prospective boundary}
The failure anatomy table reuses the existing per-perturbation
\texttt{identifiable\_divergence} as reordering burden, the existing
\texttt{response\_program\_shift} as response displacement, and the existing
response-magnitude change. Response displacement is one minus cosine
similarity between matched perturbation response vectors; it is descriptive
and target-informed. Model disagreement and source uncertainty retain their
source-side definitions from the frozen model members.

Figure 5 matrices use the existing pair-state table and map stable strict
directions to a tie-aware three-state representation. A source-to-target
inversion is counted only when both states are resolved and disagree; any tie
or unresolved state is retained in a separate unresolved count. Explanatory
scatter plots use response displacement and burden, with model disagreement as
marker size. These quantities are explicitly post-hoc explanatory and cannot
enter a prospective predictor.

The prospective feature boundary is frozen to
\texttt{uq\_cosine\_disagreement}, \texttt{uq\_mean\_gene\_variance},
\texttt{uq\_effect\_norm\_variance}, \texttt{prediction\_norm},
\texttt{prediction\_sparsity} and \texttt{prediction\_concentration}. Target
outcomes, response displacement, effect magnitude, target risk and realized
cell counts are excluded from the feature matrix. Leave-label-out evaluation
uses target outcomes only after predictions are made. Requested
cross-dataset directions remain blocked when canonical per-label outcomes are
unavailable; no zero imputation is performed.

\section{PTL deployment probe}
The deployment feature matrix separates uncertainty ($U$), prediction geometry
($P$), training support ($S$), novelty ($N$) and declared context descriptors
($C$). Environment and predictor identifiers, fidelity, continuous risk,
realized cell counts, QC outcomes, target reproducibility and reference
membership are excluded. Normalizers, nearest-neighbour transforms and
categorical encoders are fit on calibration-side rows only. The information
ladder is $U\rightarrow U+P\rightarrow U+P+S\rightarrow U+P+S+N\rightarrow
U+P+S+N+C$. The source-selection gate falls back to uncertainty-only when its
validation-only acceptance rule is not met; its selected-source negative
transfer rate is 41.7\% among non-fallback decisions. It is not a safety
mechanism.

\section{Reproducibility, manifests and validation}

reproducibility; reviewable CSV and JSON manifests carry their checksums.

Validation consists of the full pytest suite, Python compile checks, the
canonical artifact audit, narrative QA, figure audit, manuscript warning checks,
main and supplement LaTeX compilation, and the final scientific lock report.

modern-model contracts and negative excess estimates explicit. No validation
step changes a split, metric, context, budget or target-blindness boundary.
