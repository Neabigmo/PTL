\section{Ordering estimands}
For perturbation pair $c=(p,q)$, context $e$, and measurement replicate $r$,
the fixed-predictor risk difference defines
\[
 Z_{c,e}^{(r)}=\operatorname{sign}(R_{p,e}^{(r)}-R_{q,e}^{(r)})
 \in\{-1,0,+1\}.
\]
The probability vector
\[
 \pi_{c,e}=\left[\Pr(Z=-1),\Pr(Z=0),\Pr(Z=+1)\right]
\]
retains ties rather than dropping them. For contexts $e_1,e_2$,
\[
 D_{\rm cross}=\mathbb{E}_c[1-\pi_{c,e_1}^{\mathsf T}\pi_{c,e_2}],\qquad
 D_{\rm within,e}=\mathbb{E}_c[1-\|\pi_{c,e}\|_2^2].
\]
Expanding the squared distance gives the exact identity
\[
 D_{\rm cross}-\frac{D_{\rm within,e_1}+D_{\rm within,e_2}}{2}
 =\frac12\mathbb{E}_c\|\pi_{c,e_1}-\pi_{c,e_2}\|_2^2\geq0.
\]
We call the left side the measurement-corrected ordering divergence. It is a
distributional quantity, not a causal effect and not the same as the
conservative max-floor excess used for operational stress testing.

The legacy strict inversion statistic is retained only as a secondary
descriptive summary:
\[
 D_{\rm strict}=\frac{\#\{(p,q):\operatorname{sign}(R_{p,e_1}-R_{q,e_1})
 \neq\operatorname{sign}(R_{p,e_2}-R_{q,e_2})\}}
 {\#\{(p,q):\text{both risk differences are strict}\}}.
\]
Ties are excluded only for this strict statistic. The perturbation-level
normalized rank displacement is
\[
 d_p=\frac{|\operatorname{rank}_{e_1}(R_p)-
 \operatorname{rank}_{e_2}(R_p)|}{n-1}.
\]

\section{Separate noise estimands}
The measurement-only estimand fixes the source predictor and resamples raw-cell
outcomes. The model-only estimand fixes the outcome and compares independent
model members. The joint estimand crosses an independent model-member pair with
independent outcome replicates. The joint surface is empirically crossed; it is
not assumed to be additive. For the operational floor,
\[
 \Delta_{\rm joint}=D_{\rm cross}-\max(D_{\rm joint,e_1},D_{\rm joint,e_2}).
\]
This max-floor convention is deliberately conservative and can be negative
even when the ordering distributions differ. It should not be substituted for
the exact corrected divergence above.

\subsection{Full-size primary resampling}
For every split seed, context, perturbation and threshold $b$, we sample with
replacement exactly the matched raw-cell count available for the two contexts,
creating two independent full-size pseudo-replicates. Controls are resampled
with the same matched-count rule. The primary Frangieh audit uses 30 seeds
($20260908$--$20260937$), 2,000 paired perturbation-bootstrap draws, three
model members, and three pinned metric entrypoints. The cell threshold $n\geq20$
defines eligibility; the $n\geq40$ split-half audit is retained as a
pre-specified sensitivity analysis. Every run checks finite outputs, exact
label coverage, disjoint or independent sampling as appropriate, and the
source-frozen prediction checksum.

\subsection{Stable-ordering cross-fit}
For each pair and metric, $p_{c,e}=\Pr_r(Z_{c,e}^{(r)}=+1)$ is estimated on
15 selection seeds and evaluated on 15 disjoint seeds. The two seed groups are
swapped. A pair is selection-stable only when its 95\% Beta$(1,1)$ posterior
interval lies entirely above or below $0.5$. We report selection-stable,
held-out-evaluable and held-out-inversion fractions without using them to
choose a metric or context pair.

\section{Controlled Frangieh Claim Lock}
The shared source-frozen surface has 243 perturbation labels across control,
co-culture and IFN$\gamma$. A global five-fold perturbation-label assignment
is fixed before model fitting. For each source context, the Ahlmann--Eltze-style
bilinear ridge predictor is fit once per fold and its predictions are scored
unchanged against each target outcome. The evaluation panel contains 8,229
genes. The full-size summary contains 27 rows (three source contexts, three
target pairs, three metrics) and the exact identity residual is below
$8.4\times10^{-17}$ for every row.

\section{Recalibration lemma}
Let $C$ be a calibration-side confidence score and let
$g(C)=\sigma(aC+b)$ with $a>0$ be a train-only Platt map. For any two strict
scores $C_i<C_j$, $g(C_i)<g(C_j)$. Thus AURC and every strict ordering are
unchanged, although Brier or log-loss can change. The 45-group counterfactual
audit records zero strict order disagreements and zero AURC change. This is a
mathematical safeguard, not a claim that all non-monotonic recalibration or
learned ranking methods are harmless.

\section{PTL deployment probe}
The headline feature matrix contains only outcome-free blocks: uncertainty
($U$), prediction geometry ($P$), training support ($S$), novelty ($N$), and
declared context descriptors ($C$). Environment and predictor identifiers,
fidelity, continuous risk, realized cell counts, QC outcomes, target
reproducibility and reference membership are excluded. Normalizers, nearest
neighbor transforms and categorical encoders are fit on calibration-side rows
only. The information ladder is
\[
 U\rightarrow U+P\rightarrow U+P+S\rightarrow U+P+S+N\rightarrow U+P+S+N+C.
\]
The row-level random-forest probe and the U-only capacity-matched baseline are
evaluated under in-domain, leave-environment-out and leave-predictor-out
protocols. The source-selection gate is evaluated separately and falls back to
U-only when its validation-only acceptance rule is not met. The gate is not a
safety mechanism: its selected-source negative-transfer rate is 41.7\% among
non-fallback decisions.

\section{Modern predictor feasibility gate}
Modern models are tracked as external feasibility contracts until all Claim
Lock conditions are satisfied. The shared source-control gene panel contains
1,536 genes selected from QC-passed source control cells and is checksum-frozen
at
\texttt{5a0113470a7ddd1912643bc00c60c0fa4b4fa346968fc5116d9196e7c3608e35}.
It is not used to retrofit a result into the current Claim Lock.

The official GEARS source imports and its CUDA external contract is runnable,
but its current artifacts are not frozen Frangieh/Nadig prediction vectors.
The official TxPert checkout (commit
\texttt{08d82eea86746b044cf7531f4ec8c5f60e1cb73f}) reproduces cached K562
single-cell-line inference on the CUDA runtime, with test fast-retrieval
$0.79968$ and test Pearson delta $0.59549$ over 38,475 perturbations. This is a
successful external contract, not a HepG2/Jurkat Claim Lock. The installed
scGPT package fails at Windows torchtext ABI loading before model construction.
The feasibility manifest records these states, checkpoint/data hashes and
explicit false Claim Lock flags. No modern result is included in the main
scientific comparison.

\section{Secondary metrics and reproducibility}
The broad benchmark reports environment-first macro averages, paired
hierarchical bootstrap intervals, risk at 50/80\% coverage, false-trust rate,
Brier score, AUROC and AUPRC. Alternative metrics reuse saved predictions and
do not refit per metric. Fixed-budget analyses reuse the same learned scores at
20, 50 and 80\% budgets. These are robustness diagnostics, not additional
training or pooled superiority claims.

The release contains fixed split assignments, prediction contracts,
outcome-free deployment features, transport matrices, source-frozen vectors,
full-size and split-half measurement/joint floors, tie-aware ordering rows,
registry and guide-coverage manifests, model feasibility checks and editable
vector figures with source-data manifests. Large local NPZ files are retained
for reproducibility but excluded from the remote repository when they exceed
hosting limits; CSV summaries, JSON provenance and checksums remain reviewable.
