\begin{abstract}
Predictor uncertainty can be severely misaligned with realized fidelity under
structured biological shift. We study a reliability
layer that combines target-free prediction geometry, training support, novelty,
and explicit biological context with a calibrated uncertainty score. This
document is a paper skeleton: quantitative claims will be populated only from
the validated PTL-v2 artifacts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Single-cell perturbation predictors are increasingly evaluated outside the
environment in which they were trained. A central practical question is whether
confidence remains calibrated when cell context, perturbation coverage, and
experimental conditions change. We propose a predictor-aware reliability layer
that preserves the separation between prediction, deployment features, and
outcomes.

\section{Problem formulation}
For a prediction $\hat{y}_i$, let $u_i$ denote model-native or ensemble
uncertainty descriptors and let $z_i$ contain target-free prediction geometry,
training support, novelty, and context. The primary contextual calibrator uses
\begin{equation}
 p_i = \sigma\!\left(\exp(a(z_i))\,\operatorname{logit}(p_{0,i}) + b(z_i)\right),
\end{equation}
where $p_{0,i}$ is a scalar uncertainty calibration fitted within the training
fold. The reliability target is defined relative to a familiar/random anchor:
$T_i = \mathbb{I}[F_i \geq \tau\,\operatorname{median}(F_{\mathrm{random}})]$.

\section{Experimental design}
We use signature-level biological instances, five-fold grouped cross-fitting,
environment-first aggregation, and an environment-then-instance paired
bootstrap. The initial PTL replay surface covers Norman, Replogle K562
essential, and Replogle RPE1. A separate official GEARS scientific pilot covers
Norman, Replogle K562 GWPS, and Replogle RPE1 with three random condition-holdout
seeds on each surface, one frozen 4,096-gene panel per dataset, and native
log-variance uncertainty. GEARS absolute-expression outputs are converted to
matched-reference deltas before evaluation. The adapter pilot is not treated as
a final PTL-v2 performance claim.

\section{Limitations and reproducibility}
The baseline replay arrays use the two or three aligned seed members available
for each signature, while the official GEARS pilot provides a separate native
UQ validation surface with condition-disjoint splits. The environment registry explicitly marks
perturbation-modality expansion as pending metadata review. The source code,
contracts, vendored style files, and provenance boundary are kept in the
project repository.
